WWI Armistice · November 11, 1918

the 11th hour

the armistice ending WWI was signed at 5am on 11/11/1918, set to take effect at 11am. over 11,000 soldiers died in those six hours because commanders kept fighting until the symbolic moment. most people need to reach the 11th hour before they act.

acts before the 11th hour. sees the cost of delay before others do.
